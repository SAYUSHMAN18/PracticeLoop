What it won't do

The things a product page usually leaves out, said plainly.

  • It won't fake an AI answer. With no AI provider configured, every feature either falls back to something deterministic and useful or tells you it's unavailable. Diagnostics and the Writing Lab have no non-AI equivalent, so they say so rather than guessing.
  • It won't let a role snoop. Being a teacher or a guardian grants nothing by itself. Every cross-user view needs an explicit action from the learner — a join code they enter, an invite they accept — and guardians only ever see a summary.
  • It won't apply to jobs for you. No automating LinkedIn or Naukri from the server. Both platforms' terms bar it, and it would mean risking your account to save a click.
  • The free demo sleeps. It's hosted on a free tier, so the first request after ~15 minutes idle takes about 50 seconds to wake the instance.
