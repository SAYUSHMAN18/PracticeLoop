Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

A plain-language description of what this personal-scale project actually does with data. It is not legal advice and is not a substitute for a review against the law where you and your users live.

What is collected

  • Account: your name, email, and a hashed password.
  • What you create: questions, notes, learning paths, diagnostic results, project write-ups, uploaded documents and resume text, and your messages to the AI mentor.
  • Activity: which questions you reviewed and when, your confidence ratings, streak and XP -- the data the spaced-repetition schedule and progress views are built from.
  • Operational logs: standard server logs, and one row per AI call (which provider, token counts, latency) used to watch cost and reliability. These are not used to profile you.

Why

Only to run the features you use. There is no advertising, no sale of data, and no third-party analytics or tracking scripts on the site.

AI processing

Generating a lesson, a diagnostic, a grade, or a mentor reply sends text to a third-party AI provider (currently Groq or Google, depending on configuration). Prompts that carry personal detail -- your typed answer, your resume, your mentor conversation -- are sent only to produce that one result. Prompts that carry none -- a topic name, a goal string -- may be cached and reused to answer an identical request from another user.

Children

PracticeLoop is usable by students, including those under the age of consent in their country. Those accounts are meant to be created and supervised by a parent, guardian, or teacher. The optional guardian link shows a summary only, and only after the student generates and shares an invite. If you believe a child set up an unsupervised account, contact us and it will be removed.

Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account (Account → Delete) removes it, including everything that references it. Operational logs age out on their own within a short window.

Your controls

  • Export: Account → Export downloads everything as one JSON file.
  • Delete: Account → Delete removes your account and data.
  • Correct: edit your profile and content directly.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions are signed cookies. The site sets a strict content-security policy and standard security headers.

Contact

Reach the operator through the address listed on the project's repository or landing page.
