1. What PracticeLoop is

PracticeLoop is a study tool: you set a goal, it builds a learning path and a spaced-repetition review queue, and an AI mentor answers questions. It is provided as-is, for personal educational use.

2. Your account

You need an email address and a password. Keep the password to yourself -- anything done from your account is treated as done by you. Tell us if you think someone else has access.

You must be old enough to consent to processing your own data where you live (13 in the United States, 16 in much of the EU, 18 in some places). If you are younger, a parent or guardian must set up and supervise the account.

3. What you put in

Questions, notes, resume text, project write-ups, and messages to the mentor are yours. You give PracticeLoop permission to store and process them only to run the features you are using -- generating a lesson, scheduling a review, answering a question. Some prompts that contain no personal detail (a topic name, a goal string) are sent to a third-party AI provider and may be cached and reused to answer the same request from someone else.

Do not upload anything illegal, anything that isn't yours to share, or anything designed to attack the service or other users.

4. What the AI produces

AI-generated lessons, answers, and feedback can be wrong or incomplete. Check anything important against a real source. PracticeLoop is not a substitute for a teacher, a doctor, a lawyer, or a financial adviser.

5. Availability

The service may be slow, interrupted, or discontinued. Export your data (Account → Export) if you want a copy you control.

6. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Account page; it removes your data. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms.
